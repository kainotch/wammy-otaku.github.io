Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 2026

Overview

Wammy is a free, open-source manga and novel reader for Android. We respect your privacy and are committed to protecting your personal data.

Data Collection

Wammy does not collect, store, or transmit any personal data to external servers. All your data stays on your device.

  • Your library, reading progress, and settings are stored locally on your device
  • Downloaded chapters are saved to your device's local storage
  • We do not use analytics, tracking, or advertising SDKs

Third-Party Services

Wammy may interact with third-party services that you choose to use:

  • Google Sign-In — If you choose to sign in with Google, your Google profile information (name, email, photo) is used locally for personalization. We do not store or transmit this data.
  • Tracking Services — If you connect AniList, MyAnimeList, Kitsu, or other trackers, your reading progress is synced directly between your device and those services.
  • Extension Sources — Content is fetched directly from third-party websites. We are not responsible for the privacy practices of those sources.

Firebase

Wammy uses Firebase Authentication solely for Google Sign-In functionality. No additional Firebase services (analytics, crashlytics, etc.) are enabled.

Open Source

Wammy's source code is publicly available on GitHub. You can inspect exactly what the app does with your data.

Contact

If you have questions about this privacy policy, reach out on our Discord or GitHub.
